    About this redesign

    The problems passengers face on IRCTC today, what this redesign changes, and where AI fits in.

    Problems today

    Tatkal is a race most people lose

    In a 2025 LocalCircles survey, 7 in 10 Tatkal bookers said tickets sold out within a minute, and only 4 in 10 trusted the system.

    The site struggles at peak load

    Outages around the 10 am Tatkal window recur every festival season.

    Payment success, booking failed

    Money is deducted, no ticket is issued, and the passenger waits for a refund.

    Waitlists are a guess

    A number like WL 18 says nothing about whether you will actually travel.

    Too many steps

    Search, CAPTCHA, OTP and payment, each a chance to fail at the worst moment.

    Disruptions are your problem

    If a train is cancelled, you search, rebook and chase the refund yourself.

    How AI is built in

    1. UnderstandA language model turns a sentence like “Hyderabad to Vizag this Friday, 3AC” into a filled search form.
    2. PredictModels trained on past booking data estimate each waitlist’s chance of confirmation.
    3. RankResults can be sorted by best chance of confirmation, not just time or fare.
    4. GuardTrip guard watches the booking and rebooks within rules the passenger sets at review.
    5. AssistThe assistant knows which step you’re on and what you’ve chosen, and answers in your language.
